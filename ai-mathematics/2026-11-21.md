<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">Susanne Walan and Mirela Vinerean-Bernhoff · International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education · 18 November 2026</p>
<p>This qualitative pilot followed 11 Swedish primary teachers through six months of professional development involving mathematics and science. Workshop discussions and a questionnaire suggested that teachers used generative AI chiefly to develop lesson ideas, while encountering mathematical errors, unrealistic time estimates, and a need to adapt outputs. The study offers practical considerations for teacher preparation. Its small volunteer sample and focus on teachers’ experiences do not establish effects on student learning or causal benefits of AI instruction.</p>
