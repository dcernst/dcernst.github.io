<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">Jana Gevertz and Steffen Marcus, guest post on Terence Tao’s blog · 8 November 2026</p>
<p>Gevertz and Marcus argue for curricula that teach students to evaluate AI outputs while preserving the productive struggle needed to develop mathematical understanding. They propose discussion of conceptual reasoning, procedural fluency, formalization, modeling, and communication, with coordination across disciplines that depend on mathematics. They also identify the substantial faculty time needed to redesign courses. These are questions and proposals for curricular development; the authors acknowledge that they have not yet found the right approach in their own classes.</p>

<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">MathHelm scientific committee, guest post on Terence Tao’s blog · 13 November 2026</p>
<p>The committee introduces a nonprofit forum for discussing AI-generated mathematical manuscripts. Researchers can contribute observations about correctness, readability, originality, ideas, and references without undertaking a complete review. The proposal gives partial checks and explanatory comments a shared home, potentially helping readers navigate a growing volume of generated work. This is a platform announcement; its effectiveness as a review resource remains to be established.</p>
