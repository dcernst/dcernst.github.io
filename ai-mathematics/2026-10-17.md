<p>Gowers reconsiders arguments about AI extinction risk, emphasizing the difficulty of estimating probabilities when scenarios share assumptions and bottlenecks. <p>In an interview, Moritz Hardt argues that benchmark scores are often mistaken for measurements of general capability. He specifically cautions against equating a high mathematics benchmark score with the expertise of a mathematician. <p class="ai-mathematics__meta">Proofs and Prompts · 16 October 2026</p>
<p>Hendrik De Bie reports examining 152 arXiv submissions in classical analysis and representation theory, including cross-listings, during 14–18 September. He found 53 papers disclosing mathematical use of AI and 85 without an AI statement. He recommends specifying models, tasks, affected results, and company support or privileged access. This is a small descriptive survey of disclosures in two categories, not a representative estimate of AI use across mathematics; absence of a statement does not establish absence of use.</p>
