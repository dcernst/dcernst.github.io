## Research

<article class="ai-mathematics__item">
<span class="ai-mathematics__tag">Research release</span>
<h3><a href="https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/">OpenAI releases mathematical results and Lean formalizations</a></h3>
<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">OpenAI · 6 October 2026</p>
<p>OpenAI reports a repository of results produced by an internal model, alongside revision and citation protocols. It says that formalizations of many proofs in Lean are included and that more will be added. The important distinction is between the release of candidate mathematical work and its subsequent checking, exposition, and community evaluation.</p>
</article>

<article class="ai-mathematics__item">
<span class="ai-mathematics__tag">Research infrastructure</span>
<h3><a href="https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/caltech-and-aim-build-ai-platform-with-mathematicians-for-mathematicians">Caltech and AIM announce a platform for mathematical collaboration</a></h3>
<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">Caltech · 7 October 2026</p>
<p>Caltech and the American Institute of Mathematics describe a proposed platform intended to help mathematicians organize conjectures, arguments, references, and parallel lines of work. Their stated aim is a tool designed with mathematicians, rather than a replacement for mathematical judgment. The project raises useful questions about provenance, credit, and the representation of evolving arguments.</p>
</article>

## Teaching and learning

<article class="ai-mathematics__item">
<span class="ai-mathematics__tag">Classroom evidence</span>
<h3><a href="https://www.ru.nl/en/research/research-news/learning-maths-goes-slightly-better-with-ai-but-teacher-plays-a-key-role">A longitudinal study of adaptive arithmetic software</a></h3>
<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">Radboud University · 2 October 2026</p>
<p>Radboud reports on a three-year observational study of 7,885 Dutch primary-school pupils. Pupils using an adaptive system showed modestly stronger growth in mathematics performance on average, with larger effects reported in large and socioeconomically vulnerable schools. The university emphasizes that the software supports, rather than substitutes for, the teacher.</p>
</article>

<article class="ai-mathematics__item">
<span class="ai-mathematics__tag">Teacher preparation</span>
<h3><a href="https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/07356331261494349">AI-supported micro-teaching for prospective mathematics teachers</a></h3>
<p class="ai-mathematics__meta">Journal of Educational Computing Research · 7 October 2026</p>
<p>A study of 64 pre-service mathematics teachers examines a multi-agent simulation used for micro-teaching practice. Its results distinguish interaction patterns by participants’ initial pedagogical-content-knowledge profiles, suggesting that AI-supported training needs scaffolding responsive to prior knowledge. The article is access-restricted; this summary is based on its abstract.</p>
</article>
