Try each problem on your own first. Outside of class, you may use resources that help you learn, including books, websites, classmates, and AI tools. **Cite and attribute ideas that are not your own**, and come to class prepared to explain, defend, critique, and adapt the ideas you use. Collaboration is encouraged, but each student must submit their own work. Complete each assignment before class and include your name and the homework number. You may revise your work during class, but **use a different color for all in-class changes**; your grade is based on your effort before class. If you use a tablet, email me your annotated homework at the end of class.

You may turn in **three late homework assignments**. Unless you have made arrangements with me in advance, work turned in after class is late. See the [syllabus]({{site.baseurl}}/teaching/mat526f26/syllabus/#homework) for the grading rubric and full homework policy, and the [Rules of the Game]({{site.baseurl}}/teaching/mat526f26/syllabus/#rules-of-the-game) for guidance on using resources. Please ask questions or visit office hours when you need help.
